# Regulus — Art Direction

- **Look:** engraved, antique celestial-atlas plates. Fine linework, hatching, stipple.
- **Palette:** deep green-teal base with a glowing cyan accent (placeholders in `lib/game/palette.dart`).
- **Inspiration:** the engraved-linework mood of The Halo Effect's album art. Reference the technique, not a specific cover.
- **Plates:** 12 concept plates exist (one per sign) with rope-ring frames and planet glyphs. Cyan accents on the water signs.
- **Notes from review:** the current concepts read as neon line-glow more than engraving, so push the hatching and stipple. The ornate frames may compete with the playfield, so decide whether frames belong to level-select cards or in-game backgrounds.
- **AI assets:** fine for mood boards and composition tests. Final plates should be hand-drawn or heavily reworked. Disclose on Steam if any AI asset ships.
